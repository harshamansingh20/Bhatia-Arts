About us

A workshop, not
a print portal.

Bhatia Arts opened its doors on a side lane in Phagwara in 1983, hand-painting shop boards and cinema hoardings. Four decades later the tools have changed — flex machines, plotters, digital presses — but the work is still handled by the same family, and still checked by hand before it leaves the shop.

Most of our work comes from shopkeepers, schools, contractors and event organisers who came back a second time, and then kept coming back. That is the only marketing we have ever needed.
