About us

Part of Phagwara
for over 40 years.

What started in 1983 as a small printing business in Phagwara has grown into one of the city's familiar names for printing, signage, branding and custom solutions.

Over the years, Bhatia Arts has evolved with changing technology, businesses and customer needs — while staying rooted in the values that started it all: quality work, honest service and lasting relationships.

Four decades later, our foundation remains simple — do good work, stand by it, and keep moving forward.
